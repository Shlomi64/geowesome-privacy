# Privacy Policy

Geowesome, by Avis Labs  
Effective September 30, 2026

Geowesome is a geography learning app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your device. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app stores, and where

Your quiz results and learning progress (which quizzes you played, your scores, and how well you know each country) are saved on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive them and have no access to them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes them.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this progress in your device's own encrypted backup to your Google account, like the data of any other app, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we can't access it.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. It doesn't connect to our servers — there aren't any.

## Microphone and speech recognition

The optional Hands-free mode lets you answer quizzes by voice. Only if you turn it on and start a Hands-free quiz, the app asks for permission to use the microphone.

While a Hands-free quiz is running, the app listens for your answers using your device's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android devices, Google's). The app only receives the text of what was said, uses it to check your answer, and then discards it. The app never records, stores or sends audio.

Where your device supports it, recognition runs entirely on the device. Otherwise, your device's speech service may process the audio as described in that service's own privacy policy (for Google: policies.google.com/privacy). You can refuse or revoke the microphone permission at any time in your device settings; everything except Hands-free mode keeps working.

Questions are read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech service, which works on the device.

## Children

Geowesome doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
